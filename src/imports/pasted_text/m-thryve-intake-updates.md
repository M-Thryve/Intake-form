6. DRAG-AND-DROP TEMPLATE RULES

When the client selects Drag & Drop:

- They must choose an existing template.
- They cannot add custom layouts.
- They cannot add custom pages.
- They cannot add custom features.
- They cannot add custom workflows.
- They cannot structurally modify the template.
- They may replace only the content and assets defined by the template.
- Each template has a predetermined catalog price.
- Do not ask for a preferred budget.
- Do not ask for a budget range.
- Do not ask for a preferred launch date.
- Delivery time comes from the selected template and format.

If the client attempts customization, show:

“Custom changes are unavailable for the Drag & Drop tier. Select Custom Made for limited modifications.”

7. CUSTOM-MADE TEMPLATE RULES

When the client selects Custom Made:

- They must choose a base template.
- They may make limited modifications.
- They may change branding, content, approved colors, and selected layouts.
- They may add predefined sections and add-ons.
- They may not request completely original architecture through this tier.
- Template price plus selected modifications determines the project price.

The template gallery and preview experience should use the same structure as Drag & Drop, but display additional editable options and add-on prices.

8. ENTERPRISE RULES

When the client selects Enterprise:

- Do not require a template.

- Project Vision is mandatory.
- Preserve the existing Industry, Project Type, Project Vision, Features, Design Preferences, Budget & Timeline, and Review screens.
- Allow custom pages, workflows, roles, integrations, data requirements, security, and architecture.

Do not show the template gallery unless the client chooses to view examples for inspiration. Examples must not become a required foundation for Enterprise.
